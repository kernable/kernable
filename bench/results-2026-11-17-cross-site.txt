bench/cross-site.sh, 2026-11-17, M2 Max, everything on one machine (a site "cannot reach" what it is never given):
site A = partition p1 (control) + a reader of site B's copy; site B = partition p2 + a reader of site A's copy; one relay.

PASS  T2 p2 took the map through its site's reader of p1's copy
      (p2 had only its own site's reader as --control; it applied map epochs 1 and 2)
PASS  T1 each site's reader follows the other site's copy through the relay
PASS  T3 a command at site A's reader of p2's copy committed at p2 through the relay
      (a p1 tenant's command at that reader: wrong_partition, as it should)
PASS  T4 the SDK at site A committed a p2 tenant's command through its reader
      (map epoch 2 names site A's reader first for p2; the workers had connected to p2 before)
FAIL  T4b after a restart, with site A's reader first in the map for p2: no answer within 20 s
      (the worker for p2 now runs on site A's reader, which hands workers no commands)

What follows: the relay carries what partitions need between sites (copies, the map and trust
records through a reader, commands through a reader). What it cannot do is the map itself: it
names one set of addresses per partition for every site, so a site's own way to a partition
(its local reader) becomes everyone's, workers included. Not tested: global unique keys and
tenant moves between partitions across sites.
