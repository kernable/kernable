# Preview Access

kernable Cloud is being built. While it is in Preview Access:

- **No SLA, no on-call.** Support in business hours, best effort; no uptime is guaranteed.
- **€149 per team and month** (excluding VAT), billed monthly. What the fee includes and what
  costs extra: [usage and prices](/cloud/usage).
- **Cancel monthly.** Your team can end Preview Access at the end of any month
  ([leaving](/cloud/leaving)).
- **Alerts by mail, no on-call.** kernable mails your team when a writer is away, a reader falls
  behind, a copy fills up or a managed node is silent; kernable's side is watched by kernable,
  in business hours (see [monitoring and support](/cloud/monitoring)).
- **Servers in the EU.** Every copy and every backup is kept there.
- **Things may change** before general availability; changes that need you to act are announced
  by mail.

Your team accepts this notice before its first project (`kernable cloud teams accept-terms`).
